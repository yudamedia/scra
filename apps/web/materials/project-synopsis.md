We are redesigning the website for South Coast Residents association.

The redesigned platform should become the primary source of trusted information relating to life on Kenya's South Coast.

Rather than simply presenting information about SCRA, the website should become a destination that residents visit regularly to:

- Stay informed
- Track community issues
- Access public information
- Participate in consultations
- Engage with SCRA initiatives
- Discover community resources

The website should become an essential community service.

---

# Design Philosophy

The visual identity should reflect the unique character of Kenya's South Coast.

Design inspiration will be drawn from:

- The Indian Ocean
- Coastal forests
- White sandy beaches
- Coral reefs
- Marine conservation
- Community
- Transparency
- Professionalism

The overall experience will be:

- Modern
- Clean
- Calm
- Trustworthy
- Accessible
- Mobile-first

The interface will use generous spacing, coastal-inspired colours and high-quality photography to create a welcoming experience.

# Design System

The platform will follow a professionally developed design system based on the SCRA identity.

The design system includes:

- Brand colours inspired by the South Coast
- Modern typography
- Accessible colour contrast
- Responsive layouts
- Reusable interface components
- Consistent spacing and visual hierarchy
- Mobile-first design principles

This ensures consistency across every page while simplifying future enhancements.
