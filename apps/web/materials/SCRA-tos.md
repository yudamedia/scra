## 1. About SCRA

The South Coast Residents Association (SCRA) is a community association representing residents and stakeholders in the South Coast region of Kenya.

SCRA's activities include community representation, environmental stewardship, development and planning advocacy, infrastructure and safety initiatives, stakeholder engagement, public participation and communication of matters affecting the South Coast community.

SCRA's published materials describe its role in protecting the character and environmental quality of the South Coast while engaging residents, developers, businesses, government agencies and other stakeholders.
